CausalMotion: Structured Physical Reasoning as Keyframe and Trajectory Guidance for Training-Free Video Generation

A training-free framework that injects explicit physical reasoning into video generation through structured keyframes and object-centric trajectories.

Overview Method Results Videos

Overview

CausalMotion first performs explicit physical reasoning to decompose a text prompt into causally grounded keyframes and object-centric trajectories. These structured representations are then used as guidance to steer a pretrained video diffusion model during inference, improving physical plausibility and temporal consistency without training.

Abstract

Recent video diffusion models can generate visually compelling videos, yet they often struggle with physically grounded motion and long-horizon interactions. We present CausalMotion, a training-free framework that introduces explicit physical reasoning into video generation. Given a text prompt, CausalMotion uses a multimodal reasoning model to infer causally consistent keyframes and object-centric motion trajectories, providing structured guidance for a pretrained video diffusion model. Rather than replacing the latent representation with trajectory-conditioned features, we perform localized latent updates at selected denoising steps, allowing the model to preserve its generative prior while following the planned motion. Experiments show that CausalMotion improves physical plausibility and temporal consistency across diverse physically grounded video generation scenarios.

Method

CausalMotion separates physical reasoning from video synthesis. Given a text prompt, it first plans causally grounded keyframes and object-centric motion, and then uses these structured representations to guide a pretrained video diffusion model during inference.

Results

CausalMotion improves physical consistency while preserving the generative capabilities of pretrained video diffusion models. We evaluate it on both physics-focused benchmarks and general video quality.

Physics-Grounded Video Generation

On PhyGenBench, CausalMotion achieves strong performance across mechanics, optics, thermal, and material phenomena.

General Video Quality

Evaluation with VBench-VLM shows that the proposed guidance maintains general video quality while introducing explicit physical constraints.
